## Login Credentials

| Role | Email | Password |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Learner** (Staff) | staff@uni.edu | chai-academy |
| **Admin** (Manager) | admin@uni.edu | chai-academy |

## How to Login

1. Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/login`
2. Enter the email and password for the desired role
3. Click "Sign In"

- Admin users are redirected to `/admin` (Admin Dashboard)
- Staff/Learner users are redirected to `/dashboard` (Learner Dashboard)
